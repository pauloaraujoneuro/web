# Google Stitch Prompt — Paulo Araújo Subpages

Copy everything below into Google Stitch. If Stitch allows reference uploads,
attach the existing Paulo Araújo logo, professional portrait, and a screenshot of
the current homepage before submitting.

---

Design a cohesive set of responsive website page concepts for **Dr. Paulo
Araújo**, a Brazilian neurosurgeon practicing in **Campo Grande, Mato Grosso do
Sul**. This is an expansion of an existing personal medical website, not a new
brand and not a generic hospital portal.

Create a reusable visual system and these connected screens:

1. **Treatments hub** — `/tratamentos`
2. **Treatment detail template** — use “Lesão do plexo braquial” as the realistic
   example for `/tratamentos/lesao-plexo-braquial`
3. **Doctor profile** — `/sobre`
4. **Location detail** — `/locais-de-atendimento/campo-grande`
5. **Educational article template** — `/blog/[slug]`

Show the most important templates at both **desktop (1440 px)** and **mobile
(390 px)** widths. The screens must clearly belong to the same website and reuse
the same header, footer, cards, buttons, breadcrumbs, badges, spacing, and type
hierarchy.

## Brand direction

- Preserve the current brand palette:
  - deep blue-gray primary: `#233E4C`
  - muted steel blue secondary: `#7E97A3`
  - warm light-gray background: `#EFEFEF`
  - white and very pale blue-gray surfaces where needed
- Use **Poppins** or a very close geometric sans-serif throughout.
- The feeling should be calm, precise, highly credible, human, and quietly
  premium—appropriate for a specialist physician.
- Build visual interest with restrained radial gradients, fine technical grid or
  line textures, thin borders, generous whitespace, careful alignment, and subtle
  depth.
- Prefer editorial layouts and disciplined cards over large decorative shapes.
- Avoid a sterile hospital aesthetic, bright medical blue, glossy gradients,
  excessive pill shapes, glassmorphism, cartoon anatomy, futuristic sci-fi UI,
  generic stock hospital photos, or visual clutter.
- Use the supplied Paulo Araújo logo and portrait if assets are attached. Do not
  redesign the logo. If assets are unavailable, use clearly labeled neutral image
  placeholders rather than inventing a person or logo.

## Global shell

- Sticky header with the Paulo Araújo logo on the left.
- Desktop navigation: **Início, Sobre, Tratamentos, Campo Grande, Blog, Contato**.
- Primary header CTA: **Agendar consulta**.
- Mobile header: logo, compact menu control, and a clearly accessible appointment
  action without crowding the viewport.
- Every inner page needs breadcrumbs, one clear H1, and a contextual next step.
- Footer should include a compact brand statement, navigation, contact channels,
  Instagram, Currículo Lattes, and visible professional credential badges.
- Keep a discreet floating WhatsApp action, especially on mobile, without covering
  content or navigation.
- Maximum content width around 1180 px; article reading width around 700–760 px.
- Meet accessible contrast, visible keyboard focus, large touch targets, semantic
  hierarchy, and comfortable reading sizes. Do not use low-contrast gray body
  copy.

## Screen 1 — Treatments hub

Design a patient-friendly discovery page, not a dense service catalog.

- Eyebrow: **Tratamentos e áreas de atuação**
- H1: **Cuidado neurocirúrgico orientado à função e à qualidade de vida**
- Short introduction explaining that treatment decisions depend on individual
  clinical evaluation.
- Group treatment cards into three sections:
  - **Nervo periférico**
  - **Cirurgia da coluna**
  - **Reabilitação neurocirúrgica**
- Each card needs a concise title, a two-line patient-oriented description, and a
  subtle **Saiba mais** link.
- Use these representative topics: lesão do plexo braquial, transferência nervosa
  em tetraplegia, síndrome do túnel do carpo, síndrome cubital, hérnia de disco,
  estenose do canal vertebral, mielopatia cervical, fraturas da coluna, and
  reabilitação neurocirúrgica.
- Include a lower-page trust block about individualized assessment and a final
  **Agendar consulta** CTA.
- Make categories easy to scan without using filter controls that imply hidden or
  dynamic content.

## Screen 2 — Treatment detail template

Use **Lesão do plexo braquial** as the example. This must feel like a trustworthy
patient education page, not a sales landing page.

- Breadcrumb: **Início / Tratamentos / Lesão do plexo braquial**
- Category badge: **Nervo periférico**
- H1: **Lesão do plexo braquial**
- Calm summary explaining that evaluation considers the injury, time since onset,
  functional loss, and rehabilitation history. Keep language educational and do
  not promise outcomes.
- Above the fold: title and summary on the left; compact appointment/trust card on
  the right with **Dr. Paulo Araújo**, **Neurocirurgião**, credential placeholders,
  **Atendimento em Campo Grande – MS**, and **Agendar avaliação**.
- Long-form content sections:
  - **O que é o plexo braquial?**
  - **Quando procurar avaliação especializada?**
  - **Como é feita a avaliação?**
  - **Possibilidades de tratamento**
  - **Quando a cirurgia pode ser considerada?**
  - **Recuperação e reabilitação**
  - **Perguntas frequentes**
- Desktop: readable article column plus a restrained sticky sidebar containing an
  on-page table of contents, doctor attribution, and appointment CTA.
- Mobile: sidebar content becomes well-ordered inline cards; no sticky element may
  reduce reading space.
- Include a visible educational disclaimer and related-treatment/article cards.
- End with a strong but sober CTA: **Converse com a equipe para agendar uma
  avaliação em Campo Grande**.

## Screen 5 — Educational article template

- Breadcrumb, category, article H1, short dek, author byline, published/updated
  dates, and estimated reading time.
- Comfortable editorial body with H2/H3 hierarchy, lists, callout notes, and a
  compact table of contents.
- Author card for Dr. Paulo Araújo, educational disclaimer, visible FAQ when
  present, related treatment, related articles, and contextual appointment CTA.
- Avoid ad-like blocks and excessive interruptions within the article.

## Content and compliance constraints

- All visible UI copy must be in natural **Brazilian Portuguese**.
- Use “paciente” or neutral language; keep the tone clear, respectful, and
  non-alarmist.
- Do not invent statistics, credentials, awards, addresses, phone numbers,
  insurance coverage, prices, testimonials, ratings, patient stories, or clinical
  outcomes.
- Do not claim that surgery is always necessary, painless, minimally invasive, or
  guaranteed to restore function.
- No before-and-after imagery and no fear-based or urgency-based conversion copy.
- Make uncertainty and individualized assessment feel intentional rather than
  vague.
